# 📱 WIET Attendance - App Installation Guide

## Install as App for Better Experience!

### On Mobile (Android)
1. Open Chrome browser
2. Visit the attendance portal link
3. Tap the menu (⋮) button in the top right
4. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
5. Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install"
6. Done! ✅ A neat icon will appear on your home screen.

### On Mobile (iPhone/iPad)
1. Open **Safari browser** (Note: this only works in Safari!)
2. Visit the attendance portal link
3. Tap the Share button (⬆️) at the bottom
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" in the top right
6. Done! ✅ The icon will appear on your home screen.

### On Desktop (Windows/Mac)
1. Open Chrome or Edge browser
2. Visit the attendance portal link
3. Look for the install icon (📥) in the URL bar (right side)
4. Click "Install"
5. Confirm installation
6. Done! ✅ You now have a quick desktop shortcut.

---

## 🌟 Benefits of Installing
- ⚡ **Faster access**: No need to search for the URL every time.
- 📱 **Home screen icon**: Looks and feels like a native app.
- 🚀 **App-like experience**: Runs full-screen, without distracting browser UI.
- 💾 **Basic offline support**: View previously cached assignments even with a weak signal.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

**Q: I don't see the install option?**
A: Make sure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari). On Android, sometimes clearing your browser cache helps.

**Q: How do I uninstall the app?**
A: Long-press the app icon on your phone and select "Uninstall" or "Remove".

**Q: How do I update to a new version?**
A: The app checks for updates automatically. When a new version is available, you'll see a notification at the top asking if you want to "Update Now" or "Later".
